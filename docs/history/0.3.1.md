### 34.1 The official-effects audit's low findings and its low-confidence observations (R18–R21)

The maintainer decided on 2026-10-15 that the 0.3.0 official-effects audit's low-severity findings R18–R21 and its
three low-confidence observations are fixed in 0.3.1 (the audit: §32.14.40–§32.14.44 for R1–R17). Each was checked
against the local client (26-09-13): its prefabs, skill prefabs and buff templates through tools/local-extract, and
for four questions the client's code (GameAssembly.dll, method addresses from global-metadata.dat as in §32.14.36).
Rendered alone in the dev viewer and in the audit's battle harness before and after (local evidence only). The runtime
reads the same files (§33 changelog of 2026-10-16); no index, sim, protocol or golden change.

#### 34.1.1 诅咒娃娃's aura (R18)

Seen: 诅咒娃娃 (token_10006_vodfox_doll) never showed `vodfox_skill_02_buff`, its 3×3 ground aura.

Official: its skill sktok_vodfox_token is an AppearSkill (cast at the appearance) whose AuraAbility's `_effects` loop
is the aura; our data's skill record is PASSIVE with duration 15 — in the token's owner variants: data/tokens.json
writes only `{ skillId, bb }` at a token's top, and the full records (skillType, duration, prefabId) per index of the
owner's skill choice in `variants[owner].skill` / `.bySkill[i].skill`, the def the sim merges for the token (UnitInfo
sends that record's `index` as `skillIndex`).

Cause: the client read the top record, which has no type, so the plan's kind was 'instant'; the sim runs the doll as a
passive with a duration and sends no skill event for it, so the loop never started. The same top record also gave the
海嗣 (token_10017_skadi2_dedant) sktok_skadi2_3 whatever 浊心斯卡蒂's skill, and the 狼群, 流形, 爬行号·防护单元, 投递坐标
and 风雪之眼 one fixed skill of theirs whatever their owner's.

Now: `skills.js skillRecordOf` reads a token's record from its variants (`tokenSkillRecord`): the record whose `index`
is the unit's skillIndex, else the top record completed with its variant record's type. The doll's plan is a passive:
the aura loops from the deploy (or from the snapshot of a view built mid-battle) while it stands — the kit retreats it
after 15 s. The 海嗣 plays its owner's skill (sktok_skadi2_1 / _2 / _3) and, its token skill being PASSIVE, its aura
loops while it stands too. Battle harness (巫恋 S2's doll, a 60 s battle): `vodfox_skill_02_buff` 0 → 2 spawns (one per
deployment); 浊心斯卡蒂 S3's 海嗣 `token_skadi2_skill_03_buff` 0 → 1.

Every token goes through it (data/tokens.json: 22 records with variants, 15 with an untyped top record). Against the
sim's token def for every token × owner × owner skill that has a skill (78; 36 more have none): the record the client
takes names the sim's skill in 48 → 78 and has its type and duration in 0 → 78. What else changes: 流形
(token_10030_mlyss_wtrman) plays its owner's skill — sktok_mlyss_wtrman_1 / _2 have no effect, so only S3's 流形 plays
`token_10030_mlyss_wtrman_split_start_01` at its cast (before: every one), and its MANUAL record with a duration makes
the plan 'lasting'; 香槟炸弹 (token_10031_swire2_gdtrap)'s PASSIVE skill takes its mode M2 from the deploy, so every
bomb shows M2's ability loop `token_swire2_buff_01_start` and attacks with M2's two hit rows (before: only a bomb the
sim gives a skill window, one placed as a piece as the harness does, from that window; her S2's bombs never); battle
harness (琳琅诗怀雅 S2, a placed bomb and two of hers): `token_swire2_buff_01_start` 1 → 3. 爬行号·防护单元, 投递坐标,
风雪之眼, “耀阳”, 医疗探机 and the 狼群 get passive plans from their own record; none of their skills has a row and
the sim sends them no skill event — harness runs of each: official and procedural effects unchanged.

#### 34.1.2 The 狼群's bites (R19), and effects saved with an inactive root

Seen: the 狼群 (token_10028_vigil_wolf) bit with the generic hit; the audit expected `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` / `_2`.

Official: those two are cast rows (ON_CAST_START → ON_CAST_END, not one-shot) of the wolf's MeleeAttack on a
VigilWolfEffectEmitter (`_vigilWolfCastEffects`), each with a `_buffKey`. The client's VigilWolfCastEffectOptions.PlayEffect
plays the row only when the owner holds that buff (Entity.ContainsBuff). The 狼群领袖 talent's buffs
(config/buff_template_holder.ab): every 狼影 beyond the pack is a `vigil_wolf_t_1_enhance[holder]`, which derives a
`…[damage]` (the attack's extra hit) — `[born]` makes one at the deploy, `[trigger]` adds one every interval up to
max_stack_cnt (2) and then creates `…[trigger_override]`, which the fatal-hit listener finishes when a 狼影 is lost. So
`vigil_token_sub_attack_1` (a shadow wolf's lunge, on the wolf) plays from 2 wolves on, `_2` with all 3. The two
prefabs, like 33 others, are saved with an inactive root; the client activates an effect's GameObject as it plays it
(Effect._DelayToActiveGameObject → GameObjectUtil.SetActiveIfNecessary). The bite's own hit is no emitter row: the
attack's `_activeBuffs` put `vigil_wolf_hit_effect` on the target, whose start creates `vigil_token_attack_hit_1` /
`_2` / `_3` by the wolf's `[damage]` count (and `_extra` with 伺夜 S2's mark).

Cause: `wiring.js _rowOk` skipped every row with a `buffKey` ("no view shows it" [ASSUMED]); and `effect.js` honoured
the root's saved `active`, so those 35 prefabs drew nothing at all — besides the 狼群's, 伺夜's and 至简's attack starts
and trail, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's chainsaw glow and starts, 号角 S1's front start, 休谟斯's buff, 圣聆初雪 S1's start, 风笛 S3's
buff, the wolf's deaths, and of the 自选 picks 傀影's S1 / S2 buffs and 艾雅法拉's hit.

Now: a VigilWolf row plays while the pack holds its buff, read from the snapshot's 狼影 count (b.snap `wolves`
[left, max], `wiring.js WOLF_BUFFS` / `wolfHolds`): `…[damage]` from 2 wolves, `…[trigger_override]` at the maximum; a
row bound to another buff still never plays. A prefab's root is active when the effect plays (`compileEffect`). Battle
harness (伺夜 S3's 狼群): `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` 0 → 7 spawns in the window, drawn over each bite (the pack stays at
2 wolves there). Root fix (the same jobs on 0.3.0 and now): 至简's `malist_attack_start` / `_trail`, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's
`gvial2_chainsaw_glow_01` / `gvial2_skill_2_start_01_f_1`, 号角 S1's `horn_skill_01_start_02_02_front` are drawn at the
captures where they were not.

[ASSUMED]: the sim's 狼影 count is the client's holders + 1 (its initial 2 is `[born]`'s holder, its 至多3只 is
max_stack_cnt 2 + 1). Open: the bite's hit — `vigil_wolf_hit_effect`'s prefabs are not in the extracted set (the
extractor does not follow a mode attack's `_activeBuffs` templates), so the generic hit stays; the shadow wolves'
idle loops (`vigil_wolf_Idle_1` / `_2` with `vigil_token_sub_start_1` / `_2`) are not extracted either.

#### 34.1.3 归溟幽灵鲨 S1's target effect (R20)

Seen: `ghost2_skill_01_hit` played on 归溟幽灵鲨 (char_1023_ghost2) herself.

#### 34.1.4 Skills that give the deploy its effect (R21)

Seen: 缄默德克萨斯 (char_1028_texas2) S1 showed her own `texas2_birth_01_start` and the generic deploy pillar; S2 / S3

#### 34.1.5 The low-confidence observations: 锏, 瑕光, 风笛

**锏's normal attack** (char_4116_blkkgt, `blkkgt_attack_start_01`, "a flat, hard-edged orange chevron on the ground"):
official, no change. The two slashes are mesh particles of flat meshes (`mesh-0fac0871`, `mesh-9fdfbe6b`: z = 0) whose
node chain (a 180° turn about an axis in the board's plane) keeps them on the board; the hard edge is the material's

shader — Custom1.x, the main texture's U offset (−0.68 → +0.44 over the life), sweeps the slash along the chevron

`baoshan` (爆闪) is a size-4 additive star whose size curve shrinks to 0.17 at 70 % of its 0.65 s life and jumps to
0.76 at 77 %, white with full alpha there (ZTest off, tint 1 × the shader's 2). The orange blob during S3: not as the
client draws it. `blemsh_skill_03_buff` (horses `ma`, wings `chibang_*`, the halo `zhuguangyun`, sorting orders −7 … 0)

drew every non-flat order-0 renderer over the units. Now an effect whose root `static_offset` has z > 0 (50 prefabs:
auras, the `*_back` halves, a slash for a unit facing up) draws its order-0 renderers in the pass under the units
(`effect.js behindUnits`); she stands in front of her horses and wings, her stars (order 10) stay over her.

**风笛** (char_222_bpipe, "a vertical yellow and black needle on every hit"): not official. It is

thrust; 德克萨斯's stab streak crosses the target, 归溟幽灵鲨 S1's beam grows upward as it thins, 野鬃's sparks streak
along their flight.

#### 34.1.6 A passive skill's window at deploy (0.3.0's R10 for passives)

### 34.2 Forge import: the upstream asks U1–U6

#### 34.2.1 U1 — compact metadata

- **Seen:** Forge new-piece fields were dropped or inferred during compact conversion (upstream asks U1; checklist N160/Z99/O41).
- **Source:** Forge `docs/DATA.md`, `docs/WORKSHOP.md` and `shared/kitImports.js` at `fc4b80c712`; upstream checklist supplied for the 0.3.1 review. This is plugin interchange, not a new official battle rule.

- **Now:** optional `chess.hidden`, `chess.ids`, `art.assets`, `origin`; strict bilingual diagnostics; explicit pair validation, pool filtering, client portrait/model/voice resolution; importer preserves declared IDs and sources. - **[ASSUMED]:** Forge hidden or non-DIY invisible flag hides both forms and DIY selection; provenance does not inherit behavior; borrowed voice preferences belong to the bank. Legacy omitted fields retain defaults. Modules, new garrisons/bonds remain out of scope.
- **Tests:** `recordsMetadata.test.js`, `records.test.js`, `forgeImport.test.js`; final suite results are in the implementation report. No intended golden moves.

### 34.19 联防 bills only what the escaped wave sent in (PR #675)

- **Seen:** On a 联防 field, an enemy born during the fight — a split child (磨砻's 木制瑞印, 沉沙's 断刃), a summon, a 变身 copy — counted as one of the leaker's survivors: it raised the HUD's ×N and, when it got through, cost the leaker LP. Reported with the fix by @Misyra (PR #675, merged with the author's commit).
- **Official:** The activity tables bill each leaked enemy to its source player; they do not say whether an enemy born on the 联防 field counts. The official `levels/enemydata/enemy_database.json` talent blackboards bound offspring (`DeadSpawn.enemy_key` / `DeadSpawn.cnt`: 磨砻 2 木制瑞印, 烹泉 4). Players' tests in an NGA thread (linked in PR #675): a 次数怪 that did not burst while its owner defended, and burst while the teammate helped in the 联防, let out 次数怪 whose leaks cost no LP.
- **Cause:** `uniteSurvivors` (`server/match/unite.js`) and the HUD's `uniteLeft` (`server/sim/spec.js`) counted every `counted` enemy of the 联防 battle, runtime spawns included. The review then found that `validateClientResult` copied `uniteRuntime` after accepting a scheduled leak through `takeSource`, letting a client exempt the whole wave with verification off; the live counter still used the wider sent + offspring clamp.
- **Now:** The 联防 field tags an enemy that appears while it runs `uniteRuntime` (`server/sim/battle/spawns.js`; the escaped wave itself, the stage's own queue, is not tagged). `uniteSurvivors` and `uniteLeft` skip it. Kills, `counted` and 完美作战 read as on every other field; a normal field is unchanged. A client-run 联防 result keeps the flag only when that leak spends the offspring budget of a parent that leaker sent in (`validateClientResult` `takeDerived`); a flagged leak without offspring budget is refused (`unite runtime`) and the server re-simulates. The flag is stored only after that take, and only on a 联防 spec — a forged flag on a normal field is dropped. The result digest includes the flag, so `SP_VERIFY=all` cannot report it differently from the server's re-simulation. The live ×N is clamped to the enemies the escaped wave sent (`fields.js uniteBillBounds`, `keySourceCounts` only), the same set settlement bills.
- **[ASSUMED]:** (1) The players' test shows 次数怪 children; every other runtime spawn (summons, 变身 copies, boss phantoms) follows the same rule — no recording shows otherwise. (2) The client plausibility check uses the data offspring budget, which cannot prove a leak's origin. A key that is both a scheduled body and another enemy's child (27 such keys in the current tables) can still be flagged within that offspring cap and pay nothing; only `SP_VERIFY=all` closes that.
- **Tests:** `test/sim/hud-capsule-resolved.test.js`, `test/match/clientCombat-review.test.js` (a flagged scheduled leak without offspring budget is refused and re-simulated with verification off; a runtime child within the offspring budget is kept and not billed; overlapping keys retain separate sent and offspring budgets; a flag on a normal field is dropped), `test/match/playtest6-matchflow.test.js` (the live ×N never exceeds the sent count).

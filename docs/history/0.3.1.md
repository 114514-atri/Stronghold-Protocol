#### 34.1.1 诅咒娃娃's aura (R18)

Seen: 诅咒娃娃 (token_10006_vodfox_doll) never showed `vodfox_skill_02_buff`, its 3×3 ground aura.

Official: its skill sktok_vodfox_token is an AppearSkill (cast at the appearance) whose AuraAbility's `_effects` loop
is the aura; our data's skill record is PASSIVE with duration 15 — in the token's owner variants: data/tokens.json

owner's skill choice in `variants[owner].skill` / `.bySkill[i].skill`, the def the sim merges for the token (UnitInfo

Cause: the client read the top record, which has no type, so the plan's kind was 'instant'; the sim runs the doll as a
passive with a duration and sends no skill event for it, so the loop never started. The same top record also gave the
海嗣 (token_10017_skadi2_dedant) sktok_skadi2_3 whatever 浊心斯卡蒂's skill, and the 狼群, 流形, 爬行号·防护单元, 投递坐标

deployment); 浊心斯卡蒂 S3's 海嗣 `token_skadi2_skill_03_buff` 0 → 1.

Every token goes through it (data/tokens.json: 22 records with variants, 15 with an untyped top record). Against the

takes names the sim's skill in 48 → 78 and has its type and duration in 0 → 78. What else changes: 流形

Seen: the 狼群 (token_10028_vigil_wolf) bit with the generic hit; the audit expected `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` / `_2`.

Official: those two are cast rows (ON_CAST_START → ON_CAST_END, not one-shot) of the wolf's MeleeAttack on a
VigilWolfEffectEmitter (`_vigilWolfCastEffects`), each with a `_buffKey`. The client's VigilWolfCastEffectOptions.PlayEffect
plays the row only when the owner holds that buff (Entity.ContainsBuff). The 狼群领袖 talent's buffs

`…[damage]` (the attack's extra hit) — `[born]` makes one at the deploy, `[trigger]` adds one every interval up to
max_stack_cnt (2) and then creates `…[trigger_override]`, which the fatal-hit listener finishes when a 狼影 is lost. So
`vigil_token_sub_attack_1` (a shadow wolf's lunge, on the wolf) plays from 2 wolves on, `_2` with all 3. The two
prefabs, like 33 others, are saved with an inactive root; the client activates an effect's GameObject as it plays it
(Effect._DelayToActiveGameObject → GameObjectUtil.SetActiveIfNecessary). The bite's own hit is no emitter row: the
attack's `_activeBuffs` put `vigil_wolf_hit_effect` on the target, whose start creates `vigil_token_attack_hit_1` /
`_2` / `_3` by the wolf's `[damage]` count (and `_extra` with 伺夜 S2's mark).

Cause: `wiring.js _rowOk` skipped every row with a `buffKey` ("no view shows it" [ASSUMED]); and `effect.js` honoured
the root's saved `active`, so those 35 prefabs drew nothing at all — besides the 狼群's, 伺夜's and 至简's attack starts
and trail, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's chainsaw glow and starts, 号角 S1's front start, 休谟斯's buff, 圣聆初雪 S1's start, 风笛 S3's
buff, the wolf's deaths, and of the 自选 picks 傀影's S1 / S2 buffs and 艾雅法拉's hit.

Now: a VigilWolf row plays while the pack holds its buff, read from the snapshot's 狼影 count (b.snap `wolves`
[left, max], `wiring.js WOLF_BUFFS` / `wolfHolds`): `…[damage]` from 2 wolves, `…[trigger_override]` at the maximum; a
row bound to another buff still never plays. A prefab's root is active when the effect plays (`compileEffect`). Battle
harness (伺夜 S3's 狼群): `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` 0 → 7 spawns in the window, drawn over each bite (the pack stays at
2 wolves there). Root fix (the same jobs on 0.3.0 and now): 至简's `malist_attack_start` / `_trail`, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's
`gvial2_chainsaw_glow_01` / `gvial2_skill_2_start_01_f_1`, 号角 S1's `horn_skill_01_start_02_02_front` are drawn at the
captures where they were not.

[ASSUMED]: the sim's 狼影 count is the client's holders + 1 (its initial 2 is `[born]`'s holder, its 至多3只 is
max_stack_cnt 2 + 1). Open: the bite's hit — `vigil_wolf_hit_effect`'s prefabs are not in the extracted set (the
extractor does not follow a mode attack's `_activeBuffs` templates), so the generic hit stays; the shadow wolves'

#### 34.1.4 Skills that give the deploy its effect (R21)

#### 34.1.5 The low-confidence observations: 锏, 瑕光, 风笛

#### 34.1.6 A passive skill's window at deploy (0.3.0's R10 for passives)

Seen (the R18–R21 agent's note, confirmed by an independent review): 普罗旺斯 S1 and 琳琅诗怀雅 S1 (passive, no
duration, no official cast rows) showed the procedural gold pillar at deploy. Cause: the sim opens a passive skill

on the old branch). Golden: unchanged (client only).

### 34.2 Forge import: the upstream asks U1–U6

#### 34.2.1 U1 — compact metadata

- **Seen:** Forge new-piece fields were dropped or inferred during compact conversion (upstream asks U1; checklist N160/Z99/O41).
- **Source:** Forge `docs/DATA.md`, `docs/WORKSHOP.md` and `shared/kitImports.js` at `fc4b80c712`; upstream checklist supplied for the 0.3.1 review. This is plugin interchange, not a new official battle rule.
- **Cause:** compact records lacked visibility, explicit pair IDs, exact per-form art and official provenance.
- **Now:** optional `chess.hidden`, `chess.ids`, `art.assets`, `origin`; strict bilingual diagnostics; explicit pair validation, pool filtering, client portrait/model/voice resolution; importer preserves declared IDs and sources. A golden piece whose portrait or avatar id is a character key uses that entry's `portraitE2` / `avatarE2` when present (an entry with only the base URL keeps it; model and voice have no E2 field and stay on the composed id). A bond popup uses `visibleMembers` even when the array is empty. `art.ref` uses the asset-id pattern `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$`.
- **[ASSUMED]:** Forge hidden or non-DIY invisible flag hides both forms and DIY selection; provenance does not inherit behavior; borrowed voice preferences belong to the bank. Legacy omitted fields retain defaults. Modules, new garrisons/bonds remain out of scope.
- **Tests:** `recordsMetadata.test.js`, `records.test.js`, `forgeImport.test.js`; final suite results are in the implementation report. No intended golden moves.

#### 34.2.2 U2 — geometry imports

- **Seen/source:** Forge's import whitelist admits dir/targeting modules; its WORKSHOP table documents dirVec, offsetTile, absoluteRangeKeys, sortEnemyTargets.

### 34.6 Effects that stand, start at the weapon or come back (the maintainer's report of 2026-10-16)

The maintainer's report of 2026-10-16, playing with 官方特效 on (two official screenshots of 荒芜拉普兰德 S3, one of ours of
安洁莉娜 S2): five effects unlike the client's. Each was checked against the local client (26-09-13): its prefabs and
index through tools/local-extract and, for where the client places and turns an effect, its code (GameAssembly.dll as
in §32.14.36: `Effect._InitLocationFromEntity` and `_InitLocation`, `FollowMountPoint._FollowMountPoint`, the
projectile's main effect, `AdvancedMovement` / `ParacurveMovement`'s come-back leg). Behind them are five readings of the
files the runtime got wrong — the prefab root's transform, the rotation an effect takes where it is spawned, a
renderer's pivot, and the scaling mode's scale twice (as it is now, and per axis) — and the boomerang's way back. Each
was audited over the used set (lists per cause, renders before and after in the dev viewer and the battle harness, a
sweep of 41 operators: local evidence only). The runtime reads the same files (§33 changelog of 2026-10-16 (2)); the
index was not regenerated; no sim, protocol or golden change.

#### 34.6.1 荒芜拉普兰德 S3's seal

Seen: `whitw2_skill_03_buff_01`, the seal of 荒芜拉普兰德 (char_1038_whitw2) S3, lay on the board under her as a flat
ellipse with its tips pointing into it, and its discs did not show for their first 4.8 s.

Official (the screenshots): an upright round ring around her body, facing the camera — red and black outside, a white
rim, the dark disc behind her — with a spike out of each side.

Cause, four readings at once. (1) The spawn rotation: the prefab plays at FOOT_POINT and is authored standing, its discs
and tips quads in its own XY plane (billboards and Quad meshes of local alignment). The client's
`Effect._InitLocationFromEntity` gives an effect at FOOT_POINT, HIT_POINT or HEAD_POINT the rotation of the unit's skin
animator — the `Graphic` node's −30° about X that turns the skeleton to the camera, without the FaceSwitcher's turn; the
runtime gave it none, so the plane lay in the board. (2) The pivot: the tips are Quads with a pivot of −0.5 / −0.81 left
of the centre, their twins turned 180° about Y right of it; Unity moves a particle by pivot × size along its own axes,
so they reach outward — the runtime moved them the other way, into the disc. (3) The scaling mode's scale: the clip
`s3_buffstart` grows the discs' nodes from 0 in 0.5 s, and a local-space particle is drawn at its node's scale as it is
now; the runtime kept the scale a particle was born at, 0 for the discs' 4.8 s life. (4) The tips' nodes are scaled
1 × 0.7 (x × y); the runtime took a particle's size from the x scale alone when its system has no size3D (§34.6.6), so
the spikes were 1.43 times too tall.

Now the ring stands facing the camera around her from the first frame and the tips reach out as flat spikes (renders of
the dev viewer and of the battle against the screenshots). Open: the white tip (`tip01 (1)`, sorting order −12 over the
red −14 and the black −15) reaches 1.75 ring radii by the data where the screenshots show white only to about 1.3 and
red and black beyond; its size, position, pivot and order give no reason for it (the screenshots are frames of a video;
the effect may have changed since).

#### 34.6.2 能天使 S3's light bars and the standing effects

Seen: `angle_skill_03_buff`, the light bars of 能天使 (char_103_angel) S3, lay flat at her feet.

Official: like the seal, a FOOT_POINT effect authored standing: the bars stand around her body.

Cause: (1) above. Audit of the used set: 2,590 prefabs play at FOOT, HIT or HEAD (2,429 used, by 478 units); their
planar renderers (local-aligned billboards and quads, mesh particles) are authored standing 7,255 times against 514
lying flat (1,169 others). Besides the seal and the bars, 灰毫 S2's smoke (§34.6.4) stands now, 灵知 S3's ice block,
莫斯提马's numerals and 泥岩's shield disc turn with them, and the deploy ring `common_char_birth_01` — its ring authored
−150° about X, flat on the board only under the tilt — lies round around the feet (it sank half into the board). View
billboards, most hits and sparks, look the same.

Now: an effect at FOOT, HIT or HEAD takes the unit's Graphic tilt (`mounts.js spawnRotation` → 'graphic',
`graphicRotation`; the effect's `rootRot`), and FLIP_TWO_SIDE still mirrors it for a unit facing left;
MUZZLE_POINT_WITHOUT_ROTATION, NONE and the ground spawns take the identity, and so does an effect played on a point of
the ground (a kit fx's tile, a projectile's target tile: `Effect._InitLocation` sets no rotation); MUZZLE_POINT and the
Special points keep the point's rotation (§32.14.36). A FollowMountPoint with `_keepOriginRotation` keeps its spawn
location's tilt (the status loops); one on FOOT without it turns with the unit (the skeleton's root: the tilt and the
facing); a chained effect (OnPlayEmitter) takes its own spawn location's. The dev viewer places an effect the same way.

#### 34.6.3 安洁莉娜 S2's orbs

Seen (the maintainer's screenshot, after §32.14.36's fix): the orbs of 安洁莉娜 (char_291_aglina) S2 still did not leave
from her staff.

Official: a projectile's flying effect is a child of the projectile's body, which flies the path, and the client resets
its transform (`Misc.Reset`); for every other effect it sets the root's position where it plays it. A prefab root's
authored transform is no part of the picture.

#### 34.6.5 The 回环射手's boomerang

Seen: with 官方特效 on, the boomerang of a 回环射手 (跃跃, char_4100_caper) stopped at the target; the procedural one comes

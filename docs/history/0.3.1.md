#### 34.1.1 诅咒娃娃's aura (R18)

Seen: 诅咒娃娃 (token_10006_vodfox_doll) never showed `vodfox_skill_02_buff`, its 3×3 ground aura.

Official: its skill sktok_vodfox_token is an AppearSkill (cast at the appearance) whose AuraAbility's `_effects` loop
is the aura; our data's skill record is PASSIVE with duration 15 — in the token's owner variants: data/tokens.json
writes only `{ skillId, bb }` at a token's top, and the full records (skillType, duration, prefabId) per index of the
owner's skill choice in `variants[owner].skill` / `.bySkill[i].skill`, the def the sim merges for the token (UnitInfo
sends that record's `index` as `skillIndex`).

Cause: the client read the top record, which has no type, so the plan's kind was 'instant'; the sim runs the doll as a
passive with a duration and sends no skill event for it, so the loop never started. The same top record also gave the
海嗣 (token_10017_skadi2_dedant) sktok_skadi2_3 whatever 浊心斯卡蒂's skill, and the 狼群, 流形, 爬行号·防护单元, 投递坐标
and 风雪之眼 one fixed skill of theirs whatever their owner's.

Now: `skills.js skillRecordOf` reads a token's record from its variants (`tokenSkillRecord`): the record whose `index`
is the unit's skillIndex, else the top record completed with its variant record's type. The doll's plan is a passive:
the aura loops from the deploy (or from the snapshot of a view built mid-battle) while it stands — the kit retreats it
after 15 s. The 海嗣 plays its owner's skill (sktok_skadi2_1 / _2 / _3) and, its token skill being PASSIVE, its aura
loops while it stands too. Battle harness (巫恋 S2's doll, a 60 s battle): `vodfox_skill_02_buff` 0 → 2 spawns (one per
deployment); 浊心斯卡蒂 S3's 海嗣 `token_skadi2_skill_03_buff` 0 → 1.

Every token goes through it (data/tokens.json: 22 records with variants, 15 with an untyped top record). Against the
sim's token def for every token × owner × owner skill that has a skill (78; 36 more have none): the record the client
takes names the sim's skill in 48 → 78 and has its type and duration in 0 → 78. What else changes: 流形
(token_10030_mlyss_wtrman) plays its owner's skill — sktok_mlyss_wtrman_1 / _2 have no effect, so only S3's 流形 plays
`token_10030_mlyss_wtrman_split_start_01` at its cast (before: every one), and its MANUAL record with a duration makes
the plan 'lasting'; 香槟炸弹 (token_10031_swire2_gdtrap)'s PASSIVE skill takes its mode M2 from the deploy, so every
bomb shows M2's ability loop `token_swire2_buff_01_start` and attacks with M2's two hit rows (before: only a bomb the
sim gives a skill window, one placed as a piece as the harness does, from that window; her S2's bombs never); battle
harness (琳琅诗怀雅 S2, a placed bomb and two of hers): `token_swire2_buff_01_start` 1 → 3. 爬行号·防护单元, 投递坐标,
风雪之眼, “耀阳”, 医疗探机 and the 狼群 get passive plans from their own record; none of their skills has a row and
the sim sends them no skill event — harness runs of each: official and procedural effects unchanged.

[ASSUMED]: as for every PASSIVE operator skill (§33.16), a passive token skill's loops run while the unit stands. The
variant record is the first owner's, the base piece `_a` (UnitInfo names no summoner): an elite `_b` owner's record of

#### 34.1.2 The 狼群's bites (R19), and effects saved with an inactive root

Seen: the 狼群 (token_10028_vigil_wolf) bit with the generic hit; the audit expected `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` / `_2`.

Official: those two are cast rows (ON_CAST_START → ON_CAST_END, not one-shot) of the wolf's MeleeAttack on a
VigilWolfEffectEmitter (`_vigilWolfCastEffects`), each with a `_buffKey`. The client's VigilWolfCastEffectOptions.PlayEffect
plays the row only when the owner holds that buff (Entity.ContainsBuff). The 狼群领袖 talent's buffs
(config/buff_template_holder.ab): every 狼影 beyond the pack is a `vigil_wolf_t_1_enhance[holder]`, which derives a
`…[damage]` (the attack's extra hit) — `[born]` makes one at the deploy, `[trigger]` adds one every interval up to
max_stack_cnt (2) and then creates `…[trigger_override]`, which the fatal-hit listener finishes when a 狼影 is lost. So
`vigil_token_sub_attack_1` (a shadow wolf's lunge, on the wolf) plays from 2 wolves on, `_2` with all 3. The two
prefabs, like 33 others, are saved with an inactive root; the client activates an effect's GameObject as it plays it
(Effect._DelayToActiveGameObject → GameObjectUtil.SetActiveIfNecessary). The bite's own hit is no emitter row: the
attack's `_activeBuffs` put `vigil_wolf_hit_effect` on the target, whose start creates `vigil_token_attack_hit_1` /
`_2` / `_3` by the wolf's `[damage]` count (and `_extra` with 伺夜 S2's mark).

Cause: `wiring.js _rowOk` skipped every row with a `buffKey` ("no view shows it" [ASSUMED]); and `effect.js` honoured
the root's saved `active`, so those 35 prefabs drew nothing at all — besides the 狼群's, 伺夜's and 至简's attack starts
and trail, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's chainsaw glow and starts, 号角 S1's front start, 休谟斯's buff, 圣聆初雪 S1's start, 风笛 S3's
buff, the wolf's deaths, and of the 自选 picks 傀影's S1 / S2 buffs and 艾雅法拉's hit.

Now: a VigilWolf row plays while the pack holds its buff, read from the snapshot's 狼影 count (b.snap `wolves`
[left, max], `wiring.js WOLF_BUFFS` / `wolfHolds`): `…[damage]` from 2 wolves, `…[trigger_override]` at the maximum; a
row bound to another buff still never plays. A prefab's root is active when the effect plays (`compileEffect`). Battle
harness (伺夜 S3's 狼群): `vigil_token_sub_attack_1` 0 → 7 spawns in the window, drawn over each bite (the pack stays at
2 wolves there). Root fix (the same jobs on 0.3.0 and now): 至简's `malist_attack_start` / `_trail`, 百炼嘉维尔 S2's
`gvial2_chainsaw_glow_01` / `gvial2_skill_2_start_01_f_1`, 号角 S1's `horn_skill_01_start_02_02_front` are drawn at the
captures where they were not.

[ASSUMED]: the sim's 狼影 count is the client's holders + 1 (its initial 2 is `[born]`'s holder, its 至多3只 is
max_stack_cnt 2 + 1). Open: the bite's hit — `vigil_wolf_hit_effect`'s prefabs are not in the extracted set (the
extractor does not follow a mode attack's `_activeBuffs` templates), so the generic hit stays; the shadow wolves'
idle loops (`vigil_wolf_Idle_1` / `_2` with `vigil_token_sub_start_1` / `_2`) are not extracted either.

#### 34.1.3 归溟幽灵鲨 S1's target effect (R20)

Seen: `ghost2_skill_01_hit` played on 归溟幽灵鲨 (char_1023_ghost2) herself.

Official: the skill's ExchangeHp ability (AnimatedActionToTargetAbility, [uc]skills.ab skchr_ghost2_1) runs
ExchangeHpRatio (SOURCE ↔ TARGET), CreateEffect `ghost2_skill_01_start` on SOURCE and CreateEffect
`ghost2_skill_01_hit` on TARGET — the ally she swaps HP ratios with.

Cause: index v3 writes an ability's `_actions` CreateEffect uses without their target, as start rows, which play on the
owner.

Now: `FX_BINDINGS` binds the kit's 'hpShare' (id = her, `to` = the ally) to `ghost2_skill_01_hit` once on the ally (a
binding's `target` names the fx field), and a key a binding plays `at: 'target'` is no row on the owner
(`skills.js targetBound`; unmapped `bound`). Battle harness with 角峰 beside her: the hit at the ally (Unity y 1.232,
one row up) instead of at her HitPoint (0.232); the procedural pink heal flash is skipped (procedural heal 1 → 0).

Open (the same cause): 雪猎's talent (`snhunt_talent_01_hit` / `_02` on TARGET at her skill start — they still play on
her) and 真言 S1 (a 自选 pick: on PROJECTILE_TRACETARGET); the extractor should write each action's `_targetType`.

#### 34.1.4 Skills that give the deploy its effect (R21)

Seen: 缄默德克萨斯 (char_1028_texas2) S1 showed her own `texas2_birth_01_start` and the generic deploy pillar; S2 / S3
showed the pillar although their override is `common_empty`.

Official: Character.GetStartEffect returns the equipped AppearSkill's `overrideStartEffect` when it is not empty, then
the mode's `overrideStartEffect`, then the unit's own start effect; PlayBornAnimationAndEffect plays that one effect
and keeps it until StopBornAnimationAndEffect. 斯卡蒂 S2 (`skadi_skill_02_start`, looping) and 弑君者 S2 / S3 (a 自选
pick) have such an override too.

Cause: `FxWiring.deploy` played the mode's override, the unit's own or the generic effect, ignoring the skill's (the
plan played the override as a start row as well).

Now: the plan tags that start entry (`birth`); `SkillFx.birthOf` gives it to the deploy and `FxWiring.deploy` plays no
other effect: 缄默德克萨斯 S1 `texas2_birth_01_start` alone, S2 / S3 no deploy effect beside the appear strike, 斯卡蒂
S2's loop while she stands without the pillar. A passive plays it with its start one-shots; another kind's deploy
plays it and the skill's start leaves it out. `SkillFx._birth` (§32.14.44's summon dedupe) reads the same key first.
Battle harness: `common_char_birth_01` at the deploy 1 → 0 for 缄默德克萨斯 S1–S3 and 斯卡蒂 S2, the rest unchanged; the
wiring on the real index for 弑君者 (not in the harness's roster): S2 / S3 `crosly_skill_02_birth_start` /
`crosly_skill_03_birth_start` without the pillar, S1 (no override) the pillar as before.

#### 34.1.5 The low-confidence observations: 锏, 瑕光, 风笛

**锏's normal attack** (char_4116_blkkgt, `blkkgt_attack_start_01`, "a flat, hard-edged orange chevron on the ground"):
official, no change. The two slashes are mesh particles of flat meshes (`mesh-0fac0871`, `mesh-9fdfbe6b`: z = 0) whose
node chain (a 180° turn about an axis in the board's plane) keeps them on the board; the hard edge is the material's
`_MainTex` offset 0.5 (half of `trail_45_clamp`: its brightest row at the strip's edge); the custom data does reach the
shader — Custom1.x, the main texture's U offset (−0.68 → +0.44 over the life), sweeps the slash along the chevron
(zoomed renders at 0.37–0.8 s).

**瑕光** (char_423_blemsh). The large white flash at S2 +0.5 s: official, no change — `blemsh_skill_02_range`'s
`baoshan` (爆闪) is a size-4 additive star whose size curve shrinks to 0.17 at 70 % of its 0.65 s life and jumps to
0.76 at 77 %, white with full alpha there (ZTest off, tint 1 × the shader's 2). The orange blob during S3: not as the
client draws it. `blemsh_skill_03_buff` (horses `ma`, wings `chibang_*`, the halo `zhuguangyun`, sorting orders −7 … 0)
sits under a `static_offset` 0.23 deeper in the board and 0.5–1.1 tiles behind her feet; Unity sorts order 0 by
distance with the units, whose tilted bodies lie at their feet's depth, so the client draws it behind her. The runtime
drew every non-flat order-0 renderer over the units. Now an effect whose root `static_offset` has z > 0 (50 prefabs:
auras, the `*_back` halves, a slash for a unit facing up) draws its order-0 renderers in the pass under the units
(`effect.js behindUnits`); she stands in front of her horses and wings, her stars (order 10) stay over her.

**风笛** (char_222_bpipe, "a vertical yellow and black needle on every hit"): not official. It is
`bpipe_attack_01_start`'s lance flash (`baoshan`, additive, and its black twin `baoan`): stretched billboards flying
along the thrust with SizeModule X 0.5 / Y 3.5. The runtime ran a stretched billboard's X size along the velocity and
its Y size across; the client's data says Y is the stretched axis — of the used set's stretched renderers with a pivot,
2,434 set it in Y and 9 in X (plain billboards 405 / 379), and 2,130 of the 2,449 stretched systems with unequal X / Y
sizes make Y the long one. Now `geom.js` stretches by the Y size and takes the X size across: the lance runs along the
thrust; 德克萨斯's stab streak crosses the target, 归溟幽灵鲨 S1's beam grows upward as it thins, 野鬃's sparks streak
along their flight.

[ASSUMED]: Unity's stretched billboard runs the particle's Y along the velocity — from the data's pivots and sizes, not
the engine's code; the units sort at their feet's depth; an effect whose depth comes from its children's positions
alone (no `static_offset` offset) keeps the flat rule.

Tests: `test/render/vfx-audit031.test.js` (new: a token's variant record by skillIndex and the completed top record;
the doll's passive plan, its aura from the deploy and from the snapshot, gone with it; a passive token skill's mode

without the hit, the hit on the hpShare's ally, another kit's hpShare unbound; 缄默德克萨斯 S1 / S2's deploy, 斯卡蒂

#### 34.1.6 A passive skill's window at deploy (0.3.0's R10 for passives)

Seen (the R18–R21 agent's note, confirmed by an independent review): 普罗旺斯 S1 and 琳琅诗怀雅 S1 (passive, no
duration, no official cast rows) showed the procedural gold pillar at deploy. Cause: the sim opens a passive skill
without a duration for 0.5 s at deploy, and `SkillFx.skill`'s passive branch returned only what `_passive` drew, never
asking `_noBurst` as an active skill's cast does (§32.14.44). Now: `return this._passive(U, v, true) || this._noBurst(U)`
— with the unit's effects installed the official layer stands for that window; a lite install without them, and the
layer off, keep the burst. Tests: `test/render/vfx-procedural.test.js` (R10 for a passive skill, 2 — the first fails
on the old branch). Golden: unchanged (client only).

### 34.2 Forge import: the upstream asks U1–U6

#### 34.2.1 U1 — compact metadata

- **Seen:** Forge new-piece fields were dropped or inferred during compact conversion (upstream asks U1; checklist N160/Z99/O41).
- **Source:** Forge `docs/DATA.md`, `docs/WORKSHOP.md` and `shared/kitImports.js` at `fc4b80c712`; upstream checklist supplied for the 0.3.1 review. This is plugin interchange, not a new official battle rule.
- **Cause:** compact records lacked visibility, explicit pair IDs, exact per-form art and official provenance.
- **Now:** optional `chess.hidden`, `chess.ids`, `art.assets`, `origin`; strict bilingual diagnostics; explicit pair validation, pool filtering, client portrait/model/voice resolution; importer preserves declared IDs and sources. A golden piece whose portrait or avatar id is a character key uses that entry's `portraitE2` / `avatarE2` when present (an entry with only the base URL keeps it; model and voice have no E2 field and stay on the composed id). A bond popup uses `visibleMembers` even when the array is empty. `art.ref` uses the asset-id pattern `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$`.
- **[ASSUMED]:** Forge hidden or non-DIY invisible flag hides both forms and DIY selection; provenance does not inherit behavior; borrowed voice preferences belong to the bank. Legacy omitted fields retain defaults. Modules, new garrisons/bonds remain out of scope.
- **Tests:** `recordsMetadata.test.js`, `records.test.js`, `forgeImport.test.js`; final suite results are in the implementation report. No intended golden moves.

#### 34.2.2 U2 — geometry imports

- **Seen/source:** Forge's import whitelist admits dir/targeting modules; its WORKSHOP table documents dirVec, offsetTile, absoluteRangeKeys, sortEnemyTargets.
- **Cause:** our import map had no equivalent facade.
- **Now:** frozen SDK `dir` (dirVec/offsetTile), `targeting` (absoluteRangeKeys); imported own test kits exercise these through the production sandbox.
- **Still refused:** sortEnemyTargets mutates its candidate array and reads live Battle/Unit objects; tier2–6, summoner and @ops remain out of scope. Other undocumented exports stay closed.
- **[ASSUMED]:** geometry uses existing engine signatures; maximum grid length 1024, frozen results, no engine-action budget charge because no battle state is touched. Callback budgets still apply.
- **Tests:** Forge import/sandbox integration and API surface parity.

#### 34.2.3 U3 — built-in language overlays

- **Seen/source:** Forge flat `i18n/<lang>.json` could not import when we shipped the same language (upstream U3).
- **Cause:** registry treated every repeated language as a duplicate.
- **Now:** one manifest-2 plugin overlays the built-in language; UI and optional game texts preserve per-key fallback and source validation. Installation scope and per-browser language choice are unchanged; no room/simulation state changes.
- **[ASSUMED]:** first plugin-folder order wins, a second overlay stays duplicate; built-in names/chains stay authoritative, invalid/missing overlay strings/downloads use built-in values. Source zh stays excluded.
- **Tests:** registry collisions, production client UI/data loading, Forge built-in-language import. Canonical-language-collision test remains intact.

#### 34.2.4 U4–U5 — interface adaptation reports

- **Seen/source:** nine Forge mounts versus four ui slots; unsupported UI-only packs said nothing could import, and script omission shared a generic report bucket.

- **Now:** explicit needsManualAdaptation/manualPanels report with panel/mount/suggestedSlot; stable PLUGIN_FORGE_MOUNT and PLUGIN_FORGE_PANEL_SCRIPT lines. Existing aside/hud→hud.right and result footer→result.tab mappings remain.
- **[ASSUMED]:** other mounts suggest hud.right, stats.tab or room.panel for author rewrites; no claim of positional equivalence. No pack scripts are executed.

#### 34.2.5 U6 — real template media

- **Seen/source:** upstream U6 asked to test declaration-to-file delivery using our own templates.
- **Cause:** cosmetic template only referenced installed art; studio art/audio templates were empty.
- **Now:** original CC0 32×32 checkerboard PNG and 0.15-second 660 Hz synthesized OGG. Files are referenced by the cosmetic template, reused by studio creation, checked/packed/served by the normal pipeline and admitted by the release plugin-resource allowlist.
- **[ASSUMED]:** studio art/audio creation with a target fills only its first editable slot; other slots stay available for the author's files.
- **Tests:** real signatures, template strict check/pack, studio slot behavior, release allowlist. No official media included and no golden move intended.
